LANGUAGE & WRITING MODULE

Every layer of language, checked — none of the science, touched

From single-word grammar fixes to full-manuscript consistency, each tool acts only on how something is said, never on what it claims.

01 · GRAMMAR

Grammar correction

Tense, subject–verb agreement, articles, prepositions, plurals, pronouns, punctuation, spelling, capitalization, fragments, run-ons.

02 · SENTENCES

Sentence formation

Rough, non-native phrasing rebuilt into correct scientific English — the researcher approves every change.

03 · REGISTER

Scientific language improvement

Academic English, concise or formal phrasing, active/passive voice, readability, redundancy, flow — as selectable options.

04 · PARAGRAPH

Paragraph-level checking

Topic sentences, sequencing, repetition, transitions, coherence, sentence length, terminology consistency within a paragraph.

05 · TERMINOLOGY

Scientific terminology checker

"nano particle" → nanoparticle, "bio sensor" → biosensor — plus units, formulas, gene/protein notation, math notation, per discipline.

06 · CONSISTENCY

Consistency checker

Catches CeNiO₃ vs CeNiO3, UV-Vis vs UV–Vis, Fig. 1 vs Figure 1 — then applies one chosen style throughout.

CONSISTENCY CHECKER — EXAMPLES

UV-Vis→UV–Vis
